Privacy Policy

Rello · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what Rello does with your information. Rello is a fitness and nutrition app with an AI coach. It is built around a simple principle: your training and nutrition history stays on your phone, not on our servers.

Who we are

Rello is operated by Kurt Sanchez, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK data protection law, we are the data controller for the information described below.

Contact: support@rello.info

Age requirement

Rello is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account and use the app. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

What we collect, and where it goes

Stays on your device

The following is stored in encrypted storage on your phone. We cannot see it, we do not upload it, and we do not have a copy:

  • Food you log, including meals, saved foods and custom recipes
  • Training plans, logged workouts, sets, reps and weights
  • Body weight, measurements and progress photos
  • Goals, calorie and macro targets
  • Your conversations with the AI coach
  • Any data imported from Apple Health

Because this data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes it. There is no server backup. You can export a copy at any time from Settings, and we recommend you do.

Stored on our servers

When you sign in with Apple, we store a minimal account record so the app knows who you are:

  • A unique account identifier issued by Apple
  • Your email address (or Apple's private relay address, if you chose to hide it)
  • Your name, if Apple provides it at first sign-in
  • Your subscription status

This is held in our database hosted by Supabase in Ireland. Nothing about your training, nutrition or body is stored there.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Rello reads workout, activity and body measurement data from Apple Health, and may write workouts and weigh-ins back to it. This happens entirely on your device. Apple Health data is never sent to our servers.

You can withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS Health app, under Sharing.

The AI coach

When you send a message to the coach, scan a photo of food, or ask it to read a training plan, the relevant content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing, along with a summary of your recent training and nutrition so the reply is useful.

  • Anthropic processes this to generate the response and does not use it to train their models.
  • If you use spoken replies, the text of the reply is sent to ElevenLabs to generate audio.
  • Food searches query the Open Food Facts database, which receives only your search term or a barcode.

Both Anthropic and ElevenLabs are based in the United States, so this involves a transfer of personal data outside the UK. These transfers are made under the standard contractual clauses and UK addendum included in their terms.

The coach is not a medical professional and its output is not medical advice.

Subscriptions and payment

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple. We never see your card details — Apple handles payment entirely. We use RevenueCat to tell us whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and your purchase history from Apple, nothing else.

Diagnostics

When something goes wrong, the app records an error log on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it with us from Settings. If you do, the log may contain technical details about what the app was doing when it failed.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We do not show advertising or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not build profiles about you for marketing.

Health and fitness information is special category data under UK GDPR. We rely on your explicit consent to process it, and you can withdraw that consent at any time by revoking permissions or deleting your account.

How long we keep it

Account records are kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete the account record. Data held on your device is removed when you delete the app. Apple retains purchase records independently under its own policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted
  • Restrict or object to how we process it
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

For data held on your device, you can exercise most of these yourself: export it, edit it or delete the app. For anything else, email support@rello.info and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

Data on your device is held in encrypted storage. Traffic between the app and our services is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and because your training and nutrition history exists only on your phone, the security of your device and its backups matters.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
